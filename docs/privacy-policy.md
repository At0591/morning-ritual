# KindRise privacy policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

KindRise is an alarm app made by Akshay Tambe, an independent developer in Mumbai, India. This policy explains what the app keeps, what leaves your phone and what you can do about it.

## What stays on your phone

KindRise keeps these things in the app's own storage on your phone, and we never receive them:

* your alarms, snooze settings and chosen tune
* your journal: the "one small good thing" lines you write each morning
* which rituals you've done, and the mornings you kept
* your settings, such as your preferred ritual theme and bedtime reminder
* the place you chose for sunrise times (see below)

Android deletes all of this when you uninstall KindRise, and the app doesn't back it up to Google Drive. To keep your journal, use Settings > Export my journal to save or send it wherever you choose.

## Location for sunrise times

If you ask KindRise to show today's sunrise and Brahma Muhurta, it requests your approximate location once. That location is accurate to about 3 km, and the app rounds it to about 1 km. The times are worked out on your phone, and your location is never sent to us or anyone else.

You can pick a city from a list instead of sharing your location. KindRise never requests precise or background location.

## Usage statistics, only if you say yes

KindRise shares anonymous usage counts only if you agree when you first open the app. One example is how many mornings the alarm was turned off. If you say yes, the app sends these events to PostHog's EU servers. PostHog is a product analytics service. If you say no, nothing is sent.

Each event has a name, such as `alarm fired` or `ritual done`. Events can also include short labels (such as a ritual theme) and numbers (such as snooze minutes). Each one has a random ID that isn't linked to your name, email or phone number.

The app never sends your journal, anything you type, your location or your contacts. It removes free text before sending, even if a future change to the app tries to include some.

You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy. PostHog's privacy policy is at [https://posthog.com/privacy](https://posthog.com/privacy).

## Subscriptions

KindRise Premium isn't available yet. When it launches, payments will go through Google Play. Google handles your payment details under its own privacy policy.

The app will use RevenueCat to check whether you have Premium. RevenueCat will receive an anonymous app user ID, the product you bought and when it renews or ends. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at [https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## What KindRise doesn't do

* No account, sign-up, name or email.
* No ads, and no data shared with ad networks.
* No camera or microphone access.
* No selling of data, to anyone.

## Notifications and alarms

KindRise asks to send notifications and to show the alarm over your lock screen, so the alarm can wake you. On some phones, such as Vivo, iQOO, Xiaomi and OnePlus, it also asks to display over other apps for the same reason. These permissions stay on your phone. We don't see when your alarms ring.

## Children

KindRise isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

## Your rights

You can see everything KindRise keeps by opening the app. To delete it, use Settings > Reset all data or uninstall the app. Settings > Privacy turns usage statistics off.

If you shared usage statistics and want them deleted, contact us with the date you installed the app. We'll then delete the events from PostHog.

If you're in the EU or UK, the GDPR or UK GDPR gives you these rights as well. If you're in a US state with its own privacy law, those rights apply too.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. For a change that affects what leaves your phone, the app will show a notice first.
